Tips for Cooking with Ghee

Low to Medium Heat

Cook on low-medium flame to preserve the nutrients and flavor of pure ghee.

Store Properly

Keep ghee in a clean, dry container at room temperature. It lasts for months without refrigeration.

Use Sparingly

1-2 teaspoons per serving is enough. Pure ghee is nutrient-dense — a little goes a long way.
